Independent foundation

AsmAI v1 will have an independent core, informed by Firstmate and mvschwarz/openrig. It will not be a fork, extension, or runtime wrapper around either reference. No compatibility with their commands, configuration, agents, or task history is required, and no migration from an existing installation is included in v1.

Ownership and tradeoff

This choice favors control over AsmAI’s five-role model, assignment ownership and human-decision rules. AsmAI takes responsibility for its own runtime integration, durable state, recovery and verification. OpenRig remains a credible packaged alternative with CLI/HTTP/MCP and custom specifications; it was not rejected for lacking integration surfaces. No live comparison established which approach would be faster or more reliable.

Ideas do not select architecture

Both projects remain design references. Their separation of durable work, ownership, notifications, live process state and completion evidence can inform later decisions. This does not adopt their schemas, storage, supervisor, terminal manager, retry rules, permission policy or packaging. The existing hosting, work-state and interactive-CLI feasibility tickets remain authoritative for those choices.

Selective component reuse

A concrete component may be copied or adapted only after a separate explicit human decision. Present the source revision, applicable license and notice requirements, dependencies, intended contract, tests, and who maintains it and handles updates. Copied code becomes AsmAI-maintained code; no automatic upstream update policy or blanket import permission is granted. No specific component is approved now.

This boundary concerns code from the two reference systems. It does not ban ordinary third-party libraries or settle the separate skill-bundle decision. AsmAI’s own open-source license remains unselected.
