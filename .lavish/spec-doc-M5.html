M5 Concurrency and limits

M5 lets the factory run many jobs at once within its limits: worker caps and the queue, concurrent jobs and repositories, conflicts and outside commits, leaders on demand, allowance, provider failures, lost sign-in and holds, pausing, resuming and cancelling jobs, the no-CI declaration and the wait for a first check, the failed-validation limit, and the free-space floor (AssemblyAI#27).

What it adds

  1. Worker caps and the queue, first come first served, never overflowing to the other provider (AssemblyAI#27, 07).
  2. Concurrent jobs and repositories, including several jobs in one repository, with conflicts resolved by workers, outside commits merged in, rebase conventions followed until the first push, and checks that take turns (AssemblyAI#27, 05).
  3. Leaders on demand: idle leaders stopped after the grace, and a leader's provider chosen at its start (AssemblyAI#27, 02, 07).
  4. Allowance, provider failures, lost sign-in and holds, with fallback only before first dispatch and provider changes as the user's decisions (AssemblyAI#27, 07).
  5. Job pause, resume and cancel, by command or through Coordination (AssemblyAI#27, 07).
  6. The no-CI declaration and the 15-minute wait for a first check (AssemblyAI#27, 06).
  7. The failed-validation limit and the dispatch limit (AssemblyAI#27, 06, 07).
  8. The disk floor, disk use in status, and asmai job clean (AssemblyAI#27, 07).
  9. asmai status in full, with --check (07).

Builds on

M4 Conversation and intervention: the status line and catch-up that show holds and queued work, and interventions.

Demo path

S5, S6 and S7, completed (AssemblyAI#27, 00):

  1. Concurrent repositories: a feature job runs while jobs run in two other repositories; a second job in the first repository meets a conflict that a worker resolves inside an assignment; full worker caps queue assignments, shown as queued, started first come first served, and never overflowing.
