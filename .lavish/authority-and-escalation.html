The default mandate

A requested outcome authorizes ordinary work needed to deliver that outcome within the agreed scope, repository rules, resource limits, and acceptance criteria. For a tested-PR job, this includes inspection, planning, research, scoped edits, checks, commits, pushing the job branch, and opening or updating its PR. Planning-only and research-only requests authorize only those outcomes.

Merge, deployment, spending beyond a granted budget, and destructive changes outside the job require separate authorization unless an explicit standing grant already covers them. Runtime tool restrictions apply independently of the factory's mandate.

Human choice between distinct viable approaches

Ask Phillip whenever more than one distinct viable approach remains after applying agreed constraints, conventions, and applicable grants. This applies even when both approaches are reversible and a leader has a clear recommendation.

Distinct approaches differ in behavior, dependencies, interfaces, cost, maintenance, or operational consequences. Equivalent expression styles and routine choices already settled by conventions or applicable grants can proceed without another question. A leader's recommendation does not make an otherwise viable alternative non-viable.

For example, if an existing library and a custom helper both satisfy the agreed constraints, present the choice. This rule narrows the default permission to choose reversible implementation details wherever a distinct approach choice remains. Ordinary authorized execution along a settled route can continue.

Reuse of approvals

Record the exact decision or action approved, its job/repository scope, conditions, and source. Carry the relevant approval through assignments, handoffs, and resumed sessions. Reuse it while its scope and conditions remain valid.

A job approval stays with that job. Repository-wide or factory-wide reuse requires an explicit grant at that scope. Changed scope or invalidated conditions require a new decision; a handoff or restart by itself does not.

Acceptance and genuine human gates

Leaders may accept results against already-agreed, checkable criteria and report the evidence. Human input remains required when acceptance depends on an unresolved preference, changed requirement, or a workflow's explicit human review. Acceptance of a tested PR means it meets agreed review-ready criteria; it does not authorize merging.

Human grilling, wayfinder exchanges, and subjective reviews require the user's actual response. A delegated decision is a separate step and cannot resolve a ticket or skill gate that explicitly requires live human exchange.

Decision records and assumptions

For decisions affecting later work, record the accountable leader, job, mandate or grant relied on, choice, rationale and evidence, material assumptions, and conditions that would reopen the decision. Mark the source as delegated or human, retaining the actual source for human answers. Pass relevant records to workers. Routine edits do not require separate decision entries.

While a human answer is pending

Pause work dependent on the unanswered choice. Continue independent authorized work and evidence gathering within the existing mandate and limits. Coordination presents the decision request and shows what is waiting. Silence or elapsed time is never approval.

Escalate a decision once and update the request when material new evidence arrives. Notification timing, operating budgets, and scheduling remain later decisions.

Revocation and changed context

Carry current authority into assignments and re-check it before the affected consequential action. Withdrawal or a material context change invalidates the relevant permission for future affected actions. The owning leader pauses or revises affected assignments and obtains a fresh decision when needed.

Match replies to the decision version they answer; a stale reply cannot approve a changed proposal. If an action already happened or is in flight, report the actual state and authorized recovery options rather than claiming revocation undid it. Storage, delivery guarantees, and cancellation mechanics remain later decisions.
