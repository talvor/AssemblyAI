Confirm the work state and coordination resolution

Unit of work: a Job is one outcome you requested, against at most one repository, and owns its handoffs, assignments, decisions and grants. Follow-ups are new linked jobs. Store: daemon-owned SQLite with the daemon as the only writer, holding an append-only journal plus current state in one transaction. Humans use `asmai` inspection and export commands, not hand edits. The full journal is retained in v1, and size limits go to the operating limits ticket. Protocol: agents use the `asmai` CLI over a local Unix socket with a per-session credential for handoffs, results, effects, decision requests, acceptance, inbox and briefs. Each provider's permission allow-list covers `asmai`, and MCP is not required. Delivery: a durable inbox. The daemon types a one-line nudge carrying the dispatch ID only at an input-ready boundary while automation owns input. The agent's fetch is the delivery evidence, nudges may repeat, and reads are idempotent. Without a deliverable boundary, the message waits. Dispatch: one identified delivery to one session generation, moving created → nudged → delivered → working → stopped (result, blocked or decision request) or unknown. Assignments move separately: active → result submitted → accepted, or rejected and back to the same worker, or needs reconciliation, or cancelled. Only current-dispatch evidence counts. Retries: automatic only for assignments the owning leader declared side-effect-free, bounded, and always as a new dispatch. Everything else goes to reconciliation. Fencing: session generations, and the daemon rejects factory-state calls from superseded generations and terminates a superseded process group before starting its replacement. External actions are not routed through `asmai`. Orphans: at startup, processes left by a previous daemon are terminated and recorded, and their assignments need reconciliation. Workers keep running through a leader restart, and their results wait for the new generation. A cancelled job's workers stop at a boundary, and work that already happened is reported. Effects: recorded with `asmai effect` as they happen, tagged with the dispatch, and listed again in the result. They are evidence that is checked against real repository and GitHub state. Reconciliation: done by the owning leader, recorded as a delegated decision, with you asked only for distinct viable recovery approaches or actions outside the mandate. Coordination reports changed plans and effects that cannot be undone. Silence: a working dispatch with no correlated observation for a configured period becomes unknown and the leader is notified. Nothing is killed or retried automatically. Duplicate, late or out-of-order observations are journaled only, and conflicting ones lead to unknown. Context: store-generated per-job briefs are canonical for leaders, and native session resume is a bonus. Human decisions: the existing versioned decision requests and source attribution from the authority decision are stored as-is. Deferred: CLI command surface and installation go to CLI setup; timeouts, retry bounds, journal size and away-notifications go to operating limits; workspace and branch isolation go to concurrent repository work; adverse-case tests go to acceptance. Glossary gains Job, Dispatch and Reconciliation.
